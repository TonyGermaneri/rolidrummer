## Runtime and resource behavior

SSB delay/reverb for drum voices use 32 preallocated contact slots with continuous parameter changes. When full, the oldest released effect tail is replaced, preserving active contacts. Source ownership retires through bounded queues; audio rendering waits for adoption capacity rather than freeing retired assets on the audio thread.

The release benchmark sustains 64 voices across 16 contacts at 48/96 kHz and blocks of 64/256 samples. Profiles cover samples, contact effects, stereo grains, and continuous grain-window MPE with FM/scanning/effects. Latest measurements are recorded in the granulator update below and in `native/build/validation/granulator-performance.csv`. These are local workload measurements, not guarantees for every host or setting.

The grain window uses a shared quarter-sine lookup initialized off the audio thread. Its maximum error is tested below 1e-7 against SSB's formula over all four symmetries and 1,001 shapes. Cached pan coefficients are compared with the original mono/stereo laws.
